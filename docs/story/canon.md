# Story canon

Decided by Refindo, 2026-10-21. When a line in `src/flow/` disagrees with this file, fix the line (or ask Refindo to change the canon). Tone: playful, family-friendly, a little Guardians of the Galaxy. No deaths on screen.

## World
- Wormhole travel ("warp") is how ships cross great distances, like hyperspace. **Power cores** are the accelerators that make a wormhole jump possible. No cores, no jump.
- Mars was the galaxy's biggest mining colony. A containment failure (radiation) **centuries ago** killed the colony and woke the native Martians. The radiation has since burned out; the surface is safe now.
- The Galactic Council forbids landing. The public thinks the reason was forgotten. It was **buried**: a political cover-up. Kit says so at level 15.
- The colony's old **warp lines** run under the surface. The in-level **Warp Gates** are portals on those lines. Kit's scanners found them after the crash.
- The Biscuit was heading for the giant wormhole by the Earth station gate. An asteroid storm closed the safe route, so the crew took the risky one past Mars.

## Cast
- **Bosco**: young corgi pilot, brave, impulsive, funny when nervous. Ignores Kit's order, which starts the story. Plays the hero.
- **Captain Kit**: elderly sphynx. Radio only, always. Cautious, dry.
- **Capybara**: calm mechanic. Stays with the ship.
- **Ducking**: genius biotech scientist, wanted by the Galactic Police. Hid on Mars five years ago because nobody would follow him. Built his station in the old mining facility. The Martians are his army and subjects. Not redeemed. Captured at the end, never killed.
- **Martians**: instinct-driven wildlife. Ducking controls them with biotech that rewires the brain through a signal that spreads host to host like a cold. They do not steal or plan on their own.

## Plot in order
1. Crawl: Mars is forbidden. Bosco hears a distress signal (Ducking's lure).
2. Kit says don't. Bosco goes. A herd of Martians launches from the surface, sticks to the ship like octopuses and wrecks the engine. The ship crashes.
3. The engine is fixable. The cores are gone (taken by the herd on Ducking's orders). Kit sends Bosco after them. Radiation is safe. Follow the Warp Gates down.
4. Hives (levels 5, 10) can't be damaged. They go unstable and rupture after three waves, which opens a warp line below. The Maze Master (level 15) is the same: outlast it.
5. Sector 2: the shafts are centuries old but freshly maintained. Level 7: someone is rearranging the walls on purpose.
6. Level 15: the colony records show the containment failure. The Council buried it. Ducking's voice reveals his biotech, his weapon (for civilized minds, whole worlds) and the lure: five years of distress calls.
7. Sector 4: a graveyard of old colony and Council ships, and a few that are not old. What happened to those crews is **left open on purpose**.
8. Level 20: the duel. Bosco stuns Ducking. The cores are recovered.
9. Ending: cores are fine but need a few days to recalibrate. The Biscuit takes the slow way to the Earth station gate. The Galactic Police meet them there for Ducking.

## Rules for writing
- Bosses can't be destroyed. Lines about beating one say "survive" or "outlast".
- Kit never appears beside Bosco in the field.
- No "Division", no humans, no "locals" (the Martians are wildlife).
- Briefings fit two lines (about 20 words). Cutscene lines stay short.
