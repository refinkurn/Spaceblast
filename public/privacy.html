Spaceblast: Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Spaceblast has no accounts, no login, no advertising trackers and no cookies. This page lists everything the game stores or sends.

Stored on your device

Your Story progress, settings, pilot name and colour, and a random device ID are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data for the game removes them.

Sent to our server

  • Leaderboard: when you clear a Story level, the game sends your score, time, the pilot name you chose and the random device ID, so the leaderboard can show your best score. Pick a name that is not your real name.
  • Online rooms: while you play Multiplayer, your pilot name, colour and moves go through our room server to the other players in your room. Rooms are not recorded.
  • Anonymous statistics: the game counts events such as "session started", "level cleared" and "round played", with the level number and time in seconds. These carry no device ID, no name and no IP address.

Our server runs on Cloudflare. Your network address is used only for a short-lived, scrambled rate limit against spam and is not stored with your scores.

Not collected

No email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers.

Children

The game is suitable for ages 12 and up and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Removing your leaderboard entry

Scores are tied only to the random device ID. Ask the developer (Refindo) through the page where you found the game, and include your pilot name and the level.
